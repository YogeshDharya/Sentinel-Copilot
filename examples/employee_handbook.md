# Example Employee Handbook

This fictional handbook is for demonstrating the local knowledge copilot only.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 20 days of annual leave per calendar year. Leave should
be requested through the company's leave system and approved by the employee's
manager before booking travel.

## Security incident reporting

Employees must report suspected security incidents to security@example.com within
one business day. Include the affected system, the time observed, and any known
impact. Do not investigate or forward potentially malicious files.

## Travel expenses

Employees should submit expense reports within 10 business days after completing
travel. Receipts are required for individual expenses above $25.
